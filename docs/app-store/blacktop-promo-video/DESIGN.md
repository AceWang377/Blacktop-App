# Blacktop Promo Video Design

## Style Prompt

Blacktop is a fast, no-login basketball court finder. The video should feel like a night-court utility: dark, sharp, practical, and athletic without becoming loud or arcade-like. Use the app's black-green identity, real simulator screenshots, map textures, and clean product motion. The tone is confident and useful: court facts before you travel.

## Colors

- Background: `#020806`
- Panel: `#07110d`
- Ink: `#f4fff0`
- Muted ink: `#aeb9ae`
- Accent green: `#9BF35A`
- Deep green: `#003f24`

## Typography

- Headline: `Montserrat`, system sans-serif
- Body and labels: `IBM Plex Mono`, `Montserrat`, system sans-serif

## Motion

- Snappy sports utility motion: short vertical rises, soft scale, restrained glow pulses.
- Use overlapping scene entrances as transitions.
- Avoid chaotic glitch, excessive neon, or fake social/community claims.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not promise verified courts, user submissions, live pickup games, or login features.
- Do not use generic blue-purple gradients.
- Do not crop screenshots into unreadable slivers.
- Do not use stock basketball photography.
